A DATA WAREHOUSE is a centralized system used for storing and analyzing large volumes of historical 
data coming from various operational (OLTP) sources. Unlike regular databases that are optimized for
day-to-day transaction processing, a DW is designed specifically for fast querying, reporting, and 
data  analysis (OLAP) over long periods of time.

Key characteristics of a DW:

Integrated :- Combines data from different sources and systems into a consistent format.
Time-Variant :- Stores historical data, allowing trend analysis and comparisons over time.
